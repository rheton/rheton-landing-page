These guidelines help you write about Rheton and use our logo correctly, whether you're covering us in the press, listing us as a partner, or linking to us from your site. If you need something that isn't here, email **hello@rheton.io**.

## Our name

**Spelling.** Write **Rheton**, with a capital R and the rest in lowercase, in sentences and headings. The all-lowercase **rheton** appears only in the logo itself. Don't write RHETON, RheTon or Rhetan, and don't split it into two words.

**Company name.** The company behind Rheton is **Rheton, LLC**. Use the full company name in legal and formal contexts; everywhere else, just Rheton.

**Pronunciation.** The "h" is silent. Both of these are correct:

| Say it | Sounds like | IPA |
|---|---|---|
| **RET-un** | the start of "rhetoric" | /ˈrɛtən/ |
| **RAY-tone** | closer to the original Greek | /ˈreɪtoʊn/ |

**Where it comes from.** Rheton comes from the Greek **ῥητόν** (*rhēton*), "that which is spoken." It shares a root with *rhētōr*, a public speaker, and with the word *rhetoric*. Rheton is an interviewer you practice with out loud, so a name about the spoken word felt right.

**Describing Rheton.** If you need a one-line description, use: *Rheton is an AI voice interviewer for practicing technical interviews.*

**Which one to use.** Use the full logo wherever there's room. Use the mark alone only where space is tight and the Rheton name appears nearby, or where people already know it's us, such as a favicon. Use the app icon for profile pictures and square tiles.

**Color.** Show the logo in black (Ink, below) on a white or light background. On a dark background, use the app icon, or ask us for a white version at **hello@rheton.io**. Don't put the logo in other colors.

**Clear space.** Leave empty space around the logo at least as wide as the height of the wordmark's lowercase letters, such as the "o". Don't crowd it with text, other logos or the edge of the frame.

**Minimum size.** Show the full logo at least 80 px (20 mm) wide and the mark at least 16 px (4 mm) tall so it stays legible.

**Please don't:**

- Stretch, squash, rotate, skew or crop the logo.
- Change its colors, add gradients, shadows, outlines or other effects.
- Rearrange, resize or space out the mark and wordmark, or pair the mark with different text.
- Set the wordmark in another font or recreate the logo yourself.
- Place it on busy photos or patterns where it's hard to see.
- Use it in a way that suggests Rheton sponsors or endorses you, unless we've agreed to that in writing.

## How we write

We write the way a good interviewer talks: plainly, directly and kindly.

- **Clear over clever.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon for its own sake.
- **Honest.** We don't overpromise. Rheton is practice; it doesn't guarantee a job.
- **Encouraging.** Feedback should help someone improve, not make them feel small. We want you to succeed.

## Using our name and logo

You're welcome to use our name and logo to refer to Rheton, for example in an article, a review, a list of tools, or a link to our site, as long as you follow these guidelines. Don't use them in your own product or company name, domain name, or social media handle, and don't use them in a way that's misleading or implies a partnership we haven't agreed to.

We may update these guidelines from time to time. Questions, partnership requests or press inquiries: **hello@rheton.io**.
