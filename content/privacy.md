Rheton, LLC ("Rheton," "we," "us," or "our") provides an AI voice interviewer for practicing technical interviews, at rheton.io and app.rheton.io (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account information.** Your email address and password, or, if you sign in with GitHub, the basic profile details GitHub shares with us, such as your email address and name. You can also add a profile photo.
- **Resume and position details.** If you upload a resume, we extract and store its text and file name, which the interviewer uses to ask about your experience. If you paste a job or position description, we use it to tailor the interview.
- **Interview content.** Your voice while you're in an interview, the code you write and run, and your configuration choices, such as the track, stages, problems and session length.
- **Messages to us.** What you send when you contact us by email or on Discord.

**Information created when you use the Service**

- **Interview summaries.** When an interview ends, we generate and save a written summary: the problems and questions you were asked, the code you submitted, assessments of your answers, skill scores, and suggestions for what to work on. For engineering exercises, this includes the changes you made to the starter code and the result of a final test run.
- **Usage records.** When each interview starts and ends and how many minutes it used, so we can apply your plan's practice budget, plus any promo code you've redeemed.
- **Technical information.** Basic logs our systems and providers keep to run and secure the Service, such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps.

**Payment information**

If you subscribe to Plus, payment is handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID and your subscription's status, plan, and renewal or cancellation dates. We don't receive or store your full card number.

**What we don't do**

We don't record or store the audio of your interviews. Your voice is streamed in real time to our speech providers to transcribe it and to generate the interviewer's voice, and it isn't kept by us afterward. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

## How we use information

- To run interviews tailored to your resume, the role you're targeting, and the setup you choose.
- To run your code in an isolated sandbox and show you the results.
- To generate your interview summaries and let you review, compare, and rerun past sessions.
- To manage your account, plan, practice budget, promo codes, and billing.
- To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems.
- To improve the Service, such as fixing bugs and refining the interviewer.
- To communicate with you about your account, billing, and changes to the Service or this policy.

## How we share information

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, under agreements that limit their use of it, and as described below. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.

- **Real-time voice and AI.** LiveKit carries the real-time audio of your interview and gives us access to the speech and AI models below. Deepgram transcribes your speech. Cartesia generates the interviewer's voice. AI language models from Google and OpenAI receive the interview conversation and related context, such as your resume text, the problem, and your code, to run the interview and write your summary.
- **Code execution.** E2B runs your code in isolated sandboxes.
- **Hosting, accounts, and storage.** Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, and file storage.
- **Payments.** Stripe processes subscription payments.
- **Sign-in.** If you choose to sign in with GitHub, GitHub shares basic profile information with us.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it's needed to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or Rheton.
- **Business transfers.** If Rheton is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

## How long we keep information

- **Account information, resume, and interview summaries** are kept until you delete them or your account. You can delete individual sessions yourself in the app.
- **Usage and subscription records** are kept while your account is active, and billing records for as long as needed to meet tax and accounting obligations.
- **When you delete your account,** we delete your profile, resume, sessions, and related records, except where we're required to keep something by law. Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited time before it's overwritten.

Our providers keep the data they process for us under their own retention policies and their agreements with us.

## Security

We protect your information with measures such as encryption in transit, access controls that limit each account to its own data, and isolated sandboxes for running code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to **security@rheton.io**.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction.** You can view and update much of your information in the app, including your profile, resume, and saved sessions.
- **Deletion.** You can delete sessions in the app, and you can ask us to delete your account and its data.
- **Other requests.** You can ask for a copy of your personal information or for more detail about how we handle it.

To make a request, email **privacy@rheton.io** from the address on your account. We'll respond within the time required by law and won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights. We may need to verify your identity first.

**California residents.** Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose, to request deletion and correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law. You can make requests as described above, or through an authorized agent.

## Cookies and local storage

The app stores your sign-in session and some preferences, such as editor settings, in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

## Children

The Service is for people 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact **privacy@rheton.io** and we'll delete it.

## International users

Rheton is based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the Service, you understand your information will be processed there.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you by email or in the Service before they take effect, and update the date above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: **privacy@rheton.io**. Rheton, LLC, Redwood City, California.
